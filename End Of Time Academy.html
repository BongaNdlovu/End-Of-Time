About

This platform presents rigorous, Christ‑centered Bible studies with cinematic clarity. Our aim is faithfulness to Scripture, reverent craftsmanship, and practical application for everyday discipleship.

Every study is grounded in sound hermeneutics, enriched with historical context, and designed to illuminate the living Word for believers seeking deeper understanding.

Studying the Bible Using First Principles

At End of Time Academy, we approach Scripture through first principles thinking—breaking down complex theological concepts to their foundational truths, then building understanding from the ground up. This method ensures we're not simply accepting tradition or interpretation without examination, but discovering what God's Word actually says at its most fundamental level.

1

Scripture Interprets Scripture

We let the Bible be its own interpreter. Rather than imposing external frameworks, we compare Scripture with Scripture, allowing clearer passages to illuminate difficult ones. The whole counsel of God reveals the meaning of its parts.

Foundation: 2 Timothy 3:16-17, 2 Peter 1:20-21

2

Context is King

Every verse exists within historical, cultural, and literary contexts. We examine who wrote it, to whom, when, why, and what literary genre it belongs to. Understanding context prevents misinterpretation and reveals the author's intended meaning.

Foundation: 2 Timothy 2:15, Acts 17:11

3

The Plain Meaning Principle

We take Scripture in its natural, literal sense unless the passage itself indicates figurative language. God communicates to be understood, not to confuse. When the plain sense makes sense, seek no other sense.

Foundation: Nehemiah 8:8, Luke 24:27

4

Original Languages Matter

While translations are valuable, consulting Hebrew (Old Testament) and Greek (New Testament) helps us understand nuances, word meanings, and linguistic patterns that deepen our comprehension. Words carry weight, and original meanings illuminate truth.

Foundation: Ezra 7:10, Psalm 119:18

5

Progressive Revelation

God revealed His truth progressively throughout Scripture. Earlier passages lay foundations; later ones build upon and clarify. The Old Testament is revealed in the New; the New Testament is concealed in the Old. We must understand where each passage fits in God's unfolding plan.

Foundation: Hebrews 1:1-2, Luke 24:44-45

6

Christ is the Center

All Scripture ultimately points to Jesus Christ—His person, work, and glory. Every study must connect to the gospel narrative and God's redemptive plan through Christ. He is the key that unlocks all of Scripture.

Foundation: John 5:39, Luke 24:27, Colossians 1:15-20

7

Application Flows from Understanding

True application begins with accurate interpretation. We first ask, "What did this mean to the original audience?" Then we identify timeless principles and apply them faithfully to our lives today. Transformation comes through truth rightly understood and obediently applied.

Foundation: James 1:22-25, 2 Timothy 3:16-17

Our Commitment

By adhering to these first principles, we ensure that our Bible studies are rooted in truth, not tradition or speculation. We invite you to join us in this journey of discovery—examining the Scriptures with fresh eyes, humble hearts, and unwavering commitment to God's revealed Word.
